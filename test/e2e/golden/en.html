=== kickoff

Kickoff: set your starting point

Starting mid-month mixed old and new money together. The kickoff draws a line: enter each balance as it stands at the start of the kickoff day, before anything dated that day posts. Everything earlier becomes history. Budgets, projections and overdue checks start from here.

Estimates add what was scheduled between each account’s last update and the kickoff day. Anything off-schedule, like a down payment or a transfer, isn’t included, so type the real balance from your bank app when you can.
